Carbon vs. Ceramic — what’s the difference?

Ceramic Recommended

  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays
  • Best-in-class heat rejection — noticeably cooler cabin
  • Zero signal interference (GPS, phone, radio)
  • Won’t fade, bubble, or discolor — ever
  • Sharpest, clearest optical view

Carbon Included

  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays
  • Won’t fade or turn purple over time
  • Sleek matte-black finish
  • Less heat rejection than ceramic
  • No extra infrared-blocking technology

FAQ

Good to know before your appointment.

Is dark window tint legal where I live?

Tint limits vary by state. In Ohio, front side windows generally have to let in at least 50% of light, while rear side windows and the rear windshield can go darker. Michigan’s rules for the front windows are stricter. Before we install, we’ll go over what’s allowed where you drive and help you pick a shade that fits.

How long does an install take?

Most passenger vehicles are done in 1–3 hours. Full-vehicle ceramic packages or larger SUVs/trucks can take up to half a day. We’ll give you an exact estimate when you book.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Every film we install is backed by a lifetime manufacturer’s warranty for as long as you own the vehicle. It covers defects in the film itself: bubbling, peeling, cracking, delaminating, and fading or color change.

It does not cover damage from use, care, or outside causes, including scratches, nicks, or tears; cleaning with ammonia-based or abrasive products; rolling windows down before the film has cured; broken or replaced glass; and accidents or vandalism.

If you notice a problem, call or text us and we’ll take a look.

How soon can I roll my windows down?

We recommend waiting 3–5 days after install before rolling windows down, longer in cold weather, to let the adhesive fully cure.

Do you offer mobile installation?

Yes — it’s the only way we operate. We’re a 100% mobile business with no shop to visit: our fully equipped rig comes to your home, office, or job site at no extra charge within our service area. Shop drop-off is coming soon for anyone who’d rather bring the car to us.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Toledo, OH and serve the surrounding area, including Perrysburg, Maumee, Sylvania, Rossford, Swanton, Fremont, Oregon, Waterville, and Southeast Michigan (Temperance, Monroe, and nearby). Not sure if you’re in range? Start a quote and tell us your address — we’ll confirm right away.
