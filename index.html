VLT (Visible Light Transmission) is the percentage of light let through your glass. Each panel below shows the same window and seat at that shade level, so lower numbers mean darker tint and less visibility inside. We’ll always confirm your local legal limits before install.

FAQ

Good to know before your appointment.

Is dark window tint legal where I live?

Legal VLT limits vary by state and province. When you submit a quote, we automatically check your ZIP code against local law and recommend compliant shades — or explain your options if you want a medical or aftermarket exemption.

How long does an install take?

Most passenger vehicles are done in 1–3 hours. Full-vehicle ceramic packages or larger SUVs/trucks can take up to half a day. We’ll give you an exact estimate when you book.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Every film we install is covered against bubbling, peeling, and color fade for as long as you own the vehicle — no fine print, transferable with proof of purchase.

How soon can I roll my windows down?

We recommend waiting 3–5 days after install before rolling windows down, longer in cold weather, to let the adhesive fully cure.

Do you offer mobile installation?

Yes — we come to you. Select “Mobile — Come To Me” and enter your address on the quote form and we’ll confirm availability and bring the full setup to your home, office, or job site at no extra charge within our service area.
